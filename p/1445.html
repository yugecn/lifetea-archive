LOVE SHOULD @ May 08, 2006

回家路上听着MOBY的LOVE SHOULD，身体飘荡在城市的夜晚，越来越忧伤，不敢想不愿想......

so we sleep in beds we never made
holding close to love,when love should fade
holding onto this is the best thing we’llever do

the morning sun is sweet and soft on your eyes
oh,my love,you always leave me surprised
I feel my heart start to burst with all my love for you

and oh how it rains
and oh how it pours
I never could feel this way for anyone but you
and oh how it rains
and oh how it pours
I never could feel this way for anyone but you

and so we take some time and slip away
holding onto love when love should stay
holding onto you is the best thing I’ll ever do

the evening sun is swweet and soft on your eyes
oh,I’ll never everleave this place
I feel my heart start to burst with all my love for you

and oh how it rains
and oh how it pours
I never could feel this way for anyone but you
and oh how it rains
and oh how it pours
I never could feel this way for anyone but you
