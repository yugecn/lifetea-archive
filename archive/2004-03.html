2004-03-23-raining day in venice @ Mar 25, 2004

we arrived in venice yesterday evening at a raining day.it is much more colder than our expection.we left our big luggages in milano and David only wear a shirt + a leather jacket,he has already got a cold,I think.

there are a lot of people from USA and the local resident here is only about 25000,more or less,there are a lot of empty houses which owned by people from other places,where they will spend their holidays in summer.

the only tranportation thing is BOAT,as the city was built on the water and all the streets are very narrow which you can only walk on.

there are thousands of small shops where the artcraft products and watches and leather products are sold mostly,with expensive prices.but I bought a special mask for WANZI!it is so easy to be broken and I hope I can bring back without any damage.I also bought a pair of ear-rings both for MAVIS and GeZi,wait for this!

the most famous sightseeing in venice is the PIAZZA S.MARCO which is a big square with a big church.we spent half an hour there taking some pictures which are really nice.

we are now in the internet bar and we won,t go anywhere as it,s so cold outside and raining.what a bad weather in my first trip to VENICE.by the way,before using the computer here,you must show the ID(for us,it is passport).

really I love the feeling of travelling in europe to see the totally different world which I can only watch from TV and Movies before,it is something different from my imagination.but it seems that David didn,t enjoy the trip so much,and he told me today that he will be happy if I enjoy the trip,I understand acturally it is the 4th time he come here and really he has not so much interests to see the sightseeings everhwhere,but prefer a business trip to visit the customers amap.

how are you all my friends?I really miss you so much,and you?well, it,s not a long time to see you again,I have so many things to share with you.

hot sunshine day in paris-2004-03-18 @ Mar 23, 2004

so tired and hot.the tempereture in paris is more than 20 c under the sunshine at noon time.we took all the clothes which can be taken off.David told this is the 4th time he came to Paris but the 1st time he saw the sunshine.

we got up at about 10 and went to Place de la concorde which is quite near by our hotel,after a short stay of 30 minutes,we walked straight along the road(champs-elysees) to ARC DE TRIOMPHE by 20 minutes.
we passed by the LANCER shop and bought a nice bag which is new collection of 2004 as the saleslady said ,anyway,I love the color and style.

after a dinner in a chinese resterant,we went to TOUR EIFFEL by METRO.

stepping out of the church,we walked along the RIVER SEINE and passed by the Boulevard saint-michel which is also a famous sightseeing in Paris because of the story in the bible.

when we arrived in PONT NEUF,it was already close to dark,but the sightseeing was even beatiful and romantic,it is the oldest and most famous bridge in Paris with the history more than 400 years.there was also a movie” LOVE OF PONT NEUF” that I liked very much which was taken here,and I found some places that appeared in the movie which is so familiar to me.

we come back to hotel around 8 pm,and we both can not help sleeping even after taking a shower!you know,we spent about totally 6 hours on walking everywhere,incredible.

it is already near to 11 am!we have to go out of the net bar for LE MUSEE DU LOUVRE….......

TRIP IN PARIS-2004-03-17 @ Mar 23, 2004

hi,everybody,long time to see !

everything is going on well,but I met a big problem for the internet connetion to my own pc in Europe.

we stay in Germany for 2 days,1 day in koln to visit the international fair with david,and 1 day in frankfurt for sightseeings.

I tried to update my website from the beginning of my trip in Europe,but I failed!!!you may don’t believe that in these european countries,it’s so difficult to find a place to get the intnet connection,even in the HOLIDAY INN hotel,I really hate this,otherwise I can record everything and update everyday and share with you,that should have been so great!but now it seems that I can only do it after I come back.what a pity.

We will stay in Paris for 3 days and after we will go to Italy,I hope we can find some place that I can succeed to make the internet connection with my own PC.

it is much more warmer than our imagination before,it is even warmer than in Nanjing.you need only to wear a shirt and a jacket max.so we have to carry these useless beatiful winter clothes everywhere.

到达上海 @ Mar 14, 2004

现在在上海，非常累。昨晚看望父母回到家中已是将近午夜12点，才开始收拾行李，洗了澡躺下准备睡觉也快两点，今早晨7点起床，妈妈送我和大卫去火车站，一路唠唠叨叨地嘱咐安全问题。送到软席候车室门口再不能前行，于是和不轻易流露感情的老妈告别，看着妈妈挥手渐远的身影，心里有点酸酸的感觉。

午饭后，上海的表妹陪我们逛街为客人购买礼物，我为客人的太太挑选了一件真丝吊带睡衣，性感的颜色。还有许多其他的真丝制品......许多漂亮的手镯。边逛街边感叹生活在上海的MM们幸福，可以花很少的钱买到无数的好东西......

最令人头疼的是，那只酒红色的28”行李箱，从南京到上海的短短路途中已经把我的右手掌七处磨出老茧来。知道以后外出千万要选择拉杆箱，长长的拉杆可以缓解箱子重量，而我现在使用的箱子，只在侧面有个短短的把手。这个教训使得我又一次扮演了祥林嫂的角色，逢人便说。

明天13：50分汉莎航空公司的飞机飞法兰克福，二姑妈和表妹开车送我们，无可想象的旅程即将开始！

2月12日”习惯死亡” @ Mar 09, 2004

那天我们躺在旅馆的床上，你一只手盖住我小小的乳房。你说你死的时候什么东西都不会给我留下，只能留给我一个微笑。“你说吧，”你这样问我，“你要我这微笑中包含什么内容？”你那垂死的微笑是一个橡皮口袋，不论你装进多少“意义”都行。

那时我点了点头。你听见我的头发在你胸脯上擦出沙沙的声音宛如微风穿过丛树林，又如你的谎言轻轻地裂开一道逢。你仿佛觉得有一丝冷酷的笑容爬上你的嘴角。你感觉到这一丝冷笑，同时，也感觉到你的心在慢慢地烂掉。

我踮起脚尖与你吻别。我最后说的是什么？
我说：“祝你找到你灵魂的碎片！”
