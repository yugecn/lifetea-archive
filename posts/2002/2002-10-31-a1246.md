# Thanks!Raymond

![](https://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://lifetea.org/images/me-bparty-sm.jpg)看见[**Raymond**](http://www.imagestation.com/member/index.html?name=rayning)的留言，他把那天我在婚礼上拍的照片贴到了[**Tiger Cafe**](http://alumni.eecs.berkeley.edu/~rayning/tcfavorites.html)上:Christina posted these pix from her friend’s wedding she attended. Nice glimpse into modern Chinese weddings.Christina’s group blog is super popular now. Gets like 300-400 visitors a day. I hadn’t checked it for a while, but it has lots of members and people talk up a storm there! When I first knew her, she knew nothing about blogging. She was my first cowriter, back in March. She’s why I have that “translation” button in the left column!不禁有些怀念那段作为TIGER CAFE第一个[**co-writer**](http://rg.blogspot.com/2002_03_31_rg_archive.html#11333972) 的日子，因为Raymond的引领，我才想到拥有自己的Weblog。我应该感谢RAYMOND，那是一段令人难忘的时光！
