# 你知道他在说什么吗？

这是老外学习中文的笔记，可以肯定的是这个人“音译”记录中文发音的：  
Dear Tim,  
shall by too dull doll by too jack won,  
dolphin long can Jim shall by too low,  
shall by too when dull low, doll car low,  
dolphin long doll Ham Eason  
“more power!”
