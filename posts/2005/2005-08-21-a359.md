# 流动的凌晨

毫无睡意，清醒的犹如白昼，你呢？

在听Diana Krall《[The Girl in the Other Room](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/jazzrm/diana_krall/girl_in_the_other_room/02.wma)》，David Benoit & Russ Freeman的《[When She Believed In Me](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/jazzwma/Benoit_Freeman/02.wma)》，《[Swept Away](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/jazzwma/Benoit_Freeman/04.wma)》，Tom Grant & Peter White的[《Fantasy》](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/jazzwma/Smooth_Jazz/Tom_Grant_Peter_White_Fantasy.wma)，Acoustic Alchemy 的[《Say Yeah》](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/jazzwma/Acoustic_Alchemy_american_english/02.wma)，Peter White的《[Caravan of Dreams](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/jazzwma/caravan_of_dreams/01.wma)》，《[Together Again](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/jazzwma/caravan_of_dreams/02.wma)》，《[City of Lights](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/jazzwma/caravan_of_dreams/07.wma)》，《[I Do](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/poprm/nohill/Track06.rm)》- (Cherish You) (98°)，《[Ain’t No Sunshine](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/poprm/nohill/Track08.rm)》- (Lighthouse Family)，Enrique Iglesias的《[Say It](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/poprm/Enrique_Iglesias_7/03.wma)》，Boney James的《[It’s On](http://www.cooltopmusic.net/jazzwma/Boney_James_Pure/08.wma)》......

在贵州购买的小玩意和刺绣，有几张刺绣是老太太年轻出嫁时绣的，是她们压箱底的宝贝。虽然年代久远、陈旧但色彩依然鲜艳，针线清晰，让人爱不释手。有的刺绣直接从衣服上截下来，这些现在成了宝贵的传统艺术品。
